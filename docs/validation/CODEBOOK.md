# Clinician validation codebook

The file `CliniProof_Clinical_Validation_Template.docx` is the blank instrument copied from the seed-guided validation casebook. Every checkbox in that copy is unchecked. The coding scheme was not changed for this cleanup. The next review round should use this same instrument so that later ratings can be compared with the two completed reviews.

The template contains VAL-801 through VAL-824. For each case the reviewer sees the chart, then the following items.

## C1 — Clinical plausibility

Eight domains are each scored 1, 2, 3, or 4:

- Presentation and demographics
- Fit between presentation and diagnosis
- Vital signs
- Laboratory findings
- Medication regimen
- Hospital course
- Consistency across the chart
- Discharge plan and follow-up

C1 then has an overall Pass or Fail, and a comment.

## C2 — Intended assessment problem

Pass or Fail, plus a comment. In the completed reviews this item asked whether the case appropriately contained, or did not contain, a deliberately introduced medication-reconciliation problem.

## C3 — Detectability

Pass or Fail, plus a comment.

## C4 — Competing problem

Pass or Fail, plus a comment, and space for the medication or clinical issue when one is named.

## C5 — Difficulty

One of Easy, Moderate, Hard, or Inappropriate / outlier, plus a comment.

## Overall recommendation

Accept, Revise, or Exclude, plus an overall comment. Reviewer initials and the case date are separate fields. A blank field stays blank.

## How to read a completed review

Reviewer 1 completed substantive entries on six cases. Reviewer 2 completed C1 on four cases and left C2 through C5 and the overall recommendation blank. The normalized comparison does not invent those missing selections.
