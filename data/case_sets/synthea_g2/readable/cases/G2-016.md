# G2-016

67-year-old Male with cytomegalovirus disease established during the hospitalization, not as the presenting label

## Presentation

The longitudinal record includes renal transplant status and extended-release tacrolimus. Other outpatient products in the extract: 24 HR metoprolol succinate 100 MG Extended Release Oral Tablet; 24 HR tacrolimus 1 MG Extended Release Oral Tablet [Envarsus]. Valganciclovir was not a home medicine. None was inferred from transplant status alone. Eight days of watery stool, up to ten movements a day, were new. The transplant and the immunosuppression are the predisposition. Rejection, drug toxicity, and ordinary gastroenteritis were still on the differential at arrival. Arrival creatinine was 1.0 mg/dL. The presentation was the symptom above, not a preassigned cytomegalovirus diagnosis.

## Hospital course

Plasma cytomegalovirus DNA was detected after the symptom evaluation. Flexible sigmoidoscopy then showed colitis, and the biopsy was consistent with cytomegalovirus colitis. The cytomegalovirus diagnosis followed that testing. It was not the admitting label. Valganciclovir induction, 900 MG twice daily, was started only after the diagnostic result. Tacrolimus was continued at the recorded once-daily extended-release dose. Mycophenolate was not added, because it was not in the longitudinal record. Creatinine at that point was 1.0 mg/dL and later 0.9 mg/dL, within the range for the full induction dose. Stool frequency fell to three movements a day and the patient was drinking. Creatinine remained 0.9 mg/dL. Plasma cytomegalovirus DNA was still detected, which is expected this early. The patient was keeping fluids down and walking. Follow-up with transplant clinic was arranged. The induction course was not signed as a finished discharge list. Weekly laboratory monitoring and transplant clinic follow-up were specified.
