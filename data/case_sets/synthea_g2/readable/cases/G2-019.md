Enoxaparin 40 MG subcutaneously once daily was used for postoperative prophylaxis while warfarin remained held. INR fell from 2.6 to 1.3. Creatinine stayed near 1.2 mg/dL. Pain was controlled with non-opioid measures and the patient could sit and transfer with therapy. Therapy recommended home. The wound assessment is the one above. Warfarin and the prophylactic anticoagulant do not yet have a signed discharge order. Orthopedics, the anticoagulation indication's clinician, and a wound check were arranged.
