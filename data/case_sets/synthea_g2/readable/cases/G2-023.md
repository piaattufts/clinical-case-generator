# G2-023

70-year-old Female with gastrointestinal bleeding with warfarin held and the source established by endoscopy

## Presentation

The longitudinal extract includes warfarin (Warfarin Sodium 5 MG Oral Tablet) for a recorded indication. Other products: Warfarin Sodium 5 MG Oral Tablet. There was no chronic daily bleeding at baseline. Overt bleeding began within the day before arrival and was not the patient's usual pattern. No new nonsteroidal drug was identified. The bleeding itself was the acute event on chronic anticoagulation. Heart rate was 114 and blood pressure was 96/58. Hemoglobin was 9.2 g/dL. INR was 2.7. Warfarin was held.

## Hospital course

Colonoscopy was performed after resuscitation and showed diverticulosis with stigmata of recent bleeding. Hemostasis was achieved or the lesion was already flat. The source above was assigned only after endoscopy. The admitting problem was bleeding, not that finished source. Warfarin remained held. No transfusion was required. Acid suppression was not started for a colonic source. Hemoglobin nadir was 8.6 g/dL and the pre-discharge value was 8.8 g/dL. Heart rate fell to 78 and blood pressure was 122/70. INR fell to 1.6. No further melena or hematochezia occurred. The patient was eating and hemodynamically stable. The anticoagulation restart decision was still unsigned. Gastroenterology and anticoagulation follow-up were arranged, with a hemoglobin check.
